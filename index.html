The Problem

Your inbox is stealing the best hours of your day.

Catching up on email steals the first hour of every workday. You start a reply, get distracted, forget. By Thursday you've dropped three things you meant to follow up on.

  • 01
    The stolen hour
    Every workday begins with 30–60 minutes of inbox catch-up before any real work gets done. That's roughly 12.5% of payroll spent on orientation, not production.
  • 02
    Fragmented mini-versions of the same pain
    After a meeting, post-lunch, end-of-day — each transition into the inbox repeats the orientation cost. A quick glance becomes 10 distracted minutes.
  • 03
    Forgotten replies and aging drafts
    You read an email meaning to reply later and forget entirely. This isn't just a time cost — it's a trust and reputation cost. Forgetting to reply costs customers, deals, and relationships.
  • 04
    Tool-amnesia
    Without a recurring reminder, even a useful product erodes from a user's habits by week 2. They forget the URL was ever there.
How It Works

Five mechanisms. One clear head.

HeadStart doesn't reorganize your inbox or draft your replies. It shows you what matters, tells you what you've dropped, and makes sure you never forget it exists.

01 — Surface
Only what matters
Reads your mailbox, filters out commercial noise and noreply senders, then classifies the rest: action-required, meeting, status-update, or FYI. You see only items worth attention.
02 — Track
Deterministic follow-through
For each action-required item, HeadStart checks your Sent and Drafts via your mail provider's API. Status: pending, draft-started, or responded. No AI judgment — these are mailbox facts.
03 — Deliver
Push at 06:55, every morning
One push notification on your phone. One tap opens the briefing. The push also works as a daily reminder that the product exists — which is how it stays in your habit by week 4.
04 — On-demand
Fresh delta, any time
The briefing URL (or PWA on your home screen) generates a fresh delta-since-last-view every time you open it. No scheduled mid-day pushes — those fail on unpredictable workdays.
05 — Self-heal
Works even on the days you don't
If you're too busy to engage during the day, yesterday's unhandled items show up in tomorrow's morning briefing automatically. There's no scenario where things slip because you were too busy.
The under-appreciated one

HeadStart shows; it doesn't decide or do. No drafting replies, no archiving, no reorganizing your inbox. No inbox surface area added. No dashboard. No app to install. A push notification and a web page.

FAQ

Good questions deserve honest answers.

HeadStart is pre-launch. We'd rather tell you what we don't do than oversell what we do.

Will HeadStart read all my email?
HeadStart reads your inbox to surface what matters — but it filters out commercial noise and noreply senders before classifying anything. It does not store your emails, does not modify your inbox, and does not label or archive anything. Your inbox is untouched.
Is my data safe?
HeadStart connects to your inbox via your mail provider's official API (Microsoft or Google). We don't store email content beyond what's needed to generate your briefing. The long-term architecture is designed so it can run on your own infrastructure when you're ready for that.
What if I miss the morning notification?
No problem — the briefing is available on-demand all day. Open the URL (or your home screen PWA) and you'll get a fresh delta-since-last-view. And if you miss a whole day, yesterday's unhandled items roll into tomorrow's morning briefing automatically. HeadStart is forgiving of real workdays.
Does it work with Outlook and Gmail?
Yes. HeadStart is built for SMB business users on both Microsoft 365 / Outlook and Google Workspace / Gmail. The platform doesn't determine whether the product is useful for you — the pain does.
Will it draft replies or take action for me?
No. HeadStart shows; it doesn't decide or do. No AI-generated replies, no auto-archiving, no actions taken on your behalf. What you do with the briefing is entirely up to you. This is a deliberate design constraint, not a gap to fill later.
When does it launch? What's the price?
HeadStart is pre-launch. We're not sharing a timeline or pricing publicly yet — the commercial model is still being figured out for different audience segments. Leave your email and we'll have a real conversation when access is ready.
